3. Description of NotiCove

NotiCove is a notification history utility. After you grant Android's Notification access, it saves the text of notifications that Android delivers to it, stores them in an encrypted database on your device, and lets you browse, search, filter, mark favorites, copy, share, export and delete them. It also offers app exclusions, a retention setting and an optional app lock.

NotiCove is not a message-recovery, backup or monitoring service. It does not connect to your messaging accounts, does not read messages inside other apps, and cannot restore deleted messages, media or notifications that it did not capture.

NotiCove is provided free of charge and is supported by advertising.

7. Limitations of Notification Capture

NotiCove can only save what Android makes available to it while access is enabled and its listener service is running. In particular:

  • Notifications posted before access was granted, while access was turned off, or while Android had stopped or disconnected the listener are generally not captured. The only exception is that notifications still in the tray may be read when the listener reconnects.
  • Android versions, device manufacturers and battery-optimization, power-saving or "app killing" features can delay, interrupt or prevent capture.
  • Android and other apps may hide, shorten or redact content—for example, sensitive notifications, lock-screen privacy settings or one-time codes. Some apps show only "New message" or similar text.
  • Only text is saved. Images, videos, voice messages, files and other attachments are not saved. For conversation notifications, only the message lines included in the notification are saved, up to the 50 most recent.
  • When a notification is updated, NotiCove keeps the latest version of that active notification rather than every intermediate version.
  • A notification being removed from the tray does not mean that the sender deleted the message. NotiCove cannot tell whether a message was deleted, edited or read in its original app.
  • Very high notification volumes, low storage or system errors may cause some notifications not to be saved.

We do not guarantee that any particular notification, message, attachment or deleted content will be captured, saved or recoverable.

8. Data Storage and User Control

You control your data: you can exclude apps, choose a retention period (7, 30 or 90 days, or keep until you delete), mark favorites, export your history, delete single records, clear all history, or uninstall the app. Clearing history and uninstalling are permanent. NotiCove does not import exports back into the app in this version.
